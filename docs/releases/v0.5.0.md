# v0.5.0 — Daily audio digests

Vepol's day now has a voice at both ends: a morning audio digest before work
and a short evening recap right after your retro — both as listenable audio in
NotebookLM, both optional, both degrading gracefully to plain files when audio
is not connected.

## What you get

- **Morning digest, now public (`kb-morning-digest`).** Every morning, after
  your scheduled processes finish, Vepol gathers their freshest output into one
  digest file and — if the NotebookLM CLI is connected — generates an audio
  overview in a monthly notebook you can listen to before work.
- **Evening recap (`kb-morning-digest --period evening`).** Right after the
  evening retro, a short (~1–2 minute) audio recap: what got done, what is
  hanging, what mail still waits, one question for the evening. Morning and
  evening keep fully separate files and state and share only the monthly
  notebook.
- **The retro is now part of your day file.** `kb-retro` appends the retro text
  to `briefs/<today>.md` alongside the morning brief and reflection, so the day
  file tells the whole story of the day — and feeds the evening recap.
- **Audio is a bonus, never a requirement.** Without the `notebooklm` CLI (or
  on quota / rate limit / auth failure) every run keeps the digest file, skips
  the audio, and exits cleanly. Connecting NotebookLM is the consent step that
  activates audio — same philosophy as Gmail in v0.4.0.
- **Scheduled audio is tightly gated.** The previous blanket ban on background
  NotebookLM audio is replaced by a narrow allowlist: only the two digest
  processes, each bound to its exact command, may schedule audio — a process
  that merely reuses the name with a different command fails validation. The
  digest binary additionally enforces its own background guard: a scheduled
  invocation that is not the allowlisted process (or that omits the audio
  output declaration) runs file-only and makes no NotebookLM calls. Honest
  boundary: this governs Vepol's scheduler and binaries; it cannot restrict
  unrelated software on your machine.
- **Mail privacy carries through.** The evening recap is built from the
  composed day file — the bounded, minimized mail mentions your brief and retro
  already surfaced. Raw mail bodies, addresses, and envelopes are never read by
  the evening path, and any untrusted-data fencing is stripped before text
  reaches NotebookLM.

## Requirements and defaults

- Both digests are **enabled by default** on fresh installs: morning after your
  learning digest, evening after the retro. With no NotebookLM connected they
  produce digest files only.
- Audio requires the [`notebooklm` CLI](https://pypi.org/project/notebooklm-py/)
  authenticated with your Google account.
- The digest synthesis uses your configured CLI agents (Codex or Claude) and
  falls back to a deterministic packet when no agent is available — a run never
  fails because synthesis did.

## Upgrading

- **Fresh installs** get both digests automatically.
- **Existing installs**: run

  ```sh
  kb-digest-migrate --file ~/knowledge/personal/processes.yaml
  ```

  It inserts the evening digest after your retro and anchors the morning digest
  behind your last enabled morning process. It is idempotent, preserves every
  other process and chain, leaves any customized digest block untouched, and
  `--revert` removes both digest blocks. If you enable `money-radar` later,
  re-run it once to re-anchor the morning digest behind it.
