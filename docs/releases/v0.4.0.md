# v0.4.0 — Mail briefing

Vepol can now read your Gmail in the morning and evening loops and fold what
matters into your daily brief and evening retro.

## What you get

- **Mail in your daily loops.** Once Gmail is connected, a background reader
  (`kb-mail-brief`) runs before the morning brief and before the evening retro,
  turns new inbox threads into a short classified summary (needs reply /
  calendar / receipt / newsletter / noise …), and the brief and retro surface
  what actually needs your attention.
- **Read-only.** This release ships no draft, send, label, archive, or delete
  path. Mail can only be read and summarized.
- **Private by design.** Only short, bounded summaries are stored, under
  `personal/mail/briefs/` with private file permissions. Raw bodies, sender
  addresses, and recipient lists are never persisted.
- **Injection-hardened.** Email is treated as untrusted input end to end:
  summaries are minimized, strictly validated, and injected into your brief
  only inside fenced untrusted-data blocks — email text is never given
  instruction authority over your brief or your knowledge base. The honest
  boundary: the reading step itself is a model with read-only Gmail access, so
  it can be shown hostile text; but it has no write or action tools, its
  selection is mechanical (time window + recency, no content judgment), and
  everything it returns is re-validated and re-fenced before use. Full
  isolation of the reading step lands together with the future actions slice.
- **Never blocks your morning.** If Gmail is unreachable — or the reader gets a
  malformed response — the brief simply carries a one-line "mail unavailable"
  note and proceeds.

## Requirements and defaults

- Mail is **enabled by default** but reads **nothing** until you connect Gmail.
  Connecting your Gmail is the consent step that activates it.
- Real reads require the **Codex CLI with Gmail access connected in that Codex
  environment**. Without Codex or Gmail access, mail quietly reports
  unavailable and everything else works as before.
- Public integration covers the daily brief (`kb-brief`), the evening retro
  (`kb-retro`), and the orchestrator cycle's retro pass.

## Upgrading

- **Fresh installs** get the mail schedule automatically.
- **Existing installs**: run

  ```sh
  kb-mail-migrate --file ~/knowledge/personal/processes.yaml
  ```

  It is idempotent (safe to re-run), preserves your existing schedule and
  process chains, keeps your brief/retro at their usual times, and
  `--revert` restores the previous schedule exactly.

## Also in this release

- The orchestrator cycle ships its accumulated local hardening, including a
  postcondition re-read check (`_kb_orchestrator/`) that verifies a cycle's
  claimed side effects actually persisted before reporting success.
