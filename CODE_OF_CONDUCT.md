# Code of conduct

Vepol is built by people from different countries, languages and backgrounds. We want every issue, pull request and discussion here to be a place where anyone can take part without fear of harassment or ridicule.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most mistakes are honest ones.
- Critique the work, not the person. Explain why, and suggest a better way when you can.
- Respect people's time: write clearly, stay on topic, and say thank you.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone started by not knowing how things work.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, slurs, personal attacks, or sexual language and imagery.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who do so repeatedly.

To report a problem, email **vadym@nahornyi.ai**. Reports are read only by the maintainers and handled in confidence.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
